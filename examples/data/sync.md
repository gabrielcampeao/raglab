# Sync troubleshooting

Notes sync automatically whenever a device is online. A green dot next to the notebook name means everything is up to date.
If a note does not appear on another device, first check that both devices are signed in to the same account.
Sync conflicts happen when the same note is edited on two devices while offline. Orbit Notes keeps both versions and marks the copy with the word Conflict in the title.
To force a full resync, open Settings, choose Advanced and click Rebuild local cache. This downloads every note again and can take several minutes on large accounts.
The Free plan syncs attachments up to 25 MB each. Plus and Team plans sync attachments up to 250 MB each.
