# Keyboard shortcuts

Press Ctrl+N on Windows and Linux, or Cmd+N on macOS, to create a new note.
Press Ctrl+K or Cmd+K to open quick search across all notebooks.
Press Ctrl+Shift+F or Cmd+Shift+F to search inside the current notebook only.
Press Ctrl+/ or Cmd+/ to see the full list of shortcuts inside the app.
You can change any shortcut in Settings under Keyboard.
