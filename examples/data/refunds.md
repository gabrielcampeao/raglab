# Refund policy

You can request a full refund within 30 days of your first payment on any paid plan.
To request a refund, open Settings, choose Billing and click Request refund, or email billing@orbitnotes.example with your account email.
Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days.
Annual plans cancelled after the first 30 days receive a prorated refund for the unused full months.
Purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play must be refunded through those stores, because Orbit Notes does not process those payments.
