# Plans and pricing

Orbit Notes has three plans: Free, Plus and Team.
The Free plan includes up to 3 devices, 1 GB of storage and 30 days of version history.
The Plus plan costs 6 dollars per month or 60 dollars per year. It includes unlimited devices, 50 GB of storage and one year of version history.
The Team plan costs 10 dollars per user per month and adds shared notebooks, admin controls and single sign on with SAML.
Students and teachers get 50 percent off the Plus plan after verifying a school email address.
You can switch plans at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
