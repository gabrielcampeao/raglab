# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment free experience for everyone participating in `raglab`, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or location. Technical disagreements are resolved through evidence, benchmarks, and respectful critique of ideas rather than people.

## Expected behavior

* Focus discussions on concrete behavior, reproducible test cases, and measurable tradeoffs.
* Respect differing architectural viewpoints and operational constraints.
* Give constructive feedback in code reviews and accept critique gracefully.
* Credit prior research, algorithms, and contributors accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Personal attacks, insults, derogatory comments, harassment, or intimidation in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
* Publishing private information belonging to others without explicit permission.
* Deliberate disruption of technical discussions or repository maintenance.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove comments, issues, commits, or pull requests that violate these standards, and may restrict accounts from further participation when warranted. To report conduct concerns privately, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub profile channels.
